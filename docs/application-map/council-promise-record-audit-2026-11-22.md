# Council campaign promises and the meeting record

## November 22 review of all 31 positions

A result earns one point, a reversal costs one point, and a missed opportunity, a documented shortfall or an unfulfilled position costs half a point. A documented effort with an open outcome or a losing vote earns zero. Count each distinct position once. Bittner has 1 net point across 12 positions (8%), Brandt has 4.5 across 9 (50%), and Derby has 2.5 across 10 (25%). The denominator now includes Bittner’s paired East and West river apartment priority. Brandt’s two proposed rental rules remain alternatives within one position.

The review compared each campaign position with the 429 sets of available Council and committee minutes described below. It looked for opposing motions, votes to deny or repeal, named objections, changes in later decisions and who actually acted. Selected work-session and hearing recordings helped resolve speaker attribution and options discussed. The search did not include a transcript of every meeting. Unrecorded advocacy, missing minutes and ambiguous parcel descriptions limit some findings.

## How the records were checked

The original summaries used a development database with minutes from 35 meetings. This review searched 429 distinct published sets of official minutes from April 16, 2024, through November 18, 2026. They cover 108 Council meetings and work sessions and 321 meetings of other bodies. Searches included project names, locations, decisions and each member’s role. Relevant passages, later outcomes and selected packets were checked against the campaign wording. The November 2 and November 16 Council packets were available, but their minutes were missing from the archive.

A finding of follow-through requires an action attributable to the member and a material result proportionate to what they undertook. A vote awarding a contract can settle a promise to pursue a specific improvement. Whether it also achieved a broader goal depends on the documented outcome. Private outreach and missing records can leave questions open. Every unresolved promise stays in the denominator and earns zero credit.
