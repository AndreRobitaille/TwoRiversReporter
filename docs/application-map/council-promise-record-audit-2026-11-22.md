## How the records were checked

The original summaries used a development database with minutes from 35 meetings. This review searched 429 distinct published sets of official minutes from April 16, 2024, through November 18, 2026. They cover 108 Council meetings and work sessions and 321 meetings of other bodies. Searches included project names, locations, decisions and each member’s role. Relevant passages, later outcomes and selected packets were checked against the campaign wording. The November 2 and November 16 Council packets were available, but their minutes were missing from the archive.

A finding of follow-through requires an action attributable to the member and a material result proportionate to what they undertook. A vote awarding a contract can settle a promise to pursue a specific improvement. Whether it also achieved a broader goal depends on the documented outcome. Private outreach and missing records can leave questions open. Every unresolved promise stays in the denominator and earns zero credit.
