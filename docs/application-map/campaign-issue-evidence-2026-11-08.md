## Implemented boundary

Existing current official-source results supply attributed actions and issue
developments. Known people, actual service periods, approved topics and the
statement's publication date retrieve eligible candidate pairs. Topic overlap
does not publish a connection. One source's missing pairs are batched across
members, at most 20 per request, using the existing comparison records, receipts,
spending controls and two-attempt bound. No new table or scheduler is added.

The comparison receives saved campaign qualifications and exact retained source
passages, rather than repeating the entire packet. A direct result must cite its
own pair's passage, not another event in the batch. Every pair receives a result;
negative and unresolved matches are cached too. Existing vote comparison inputs
and keys retain their original contract, and completed source analyses are not
reopened for the new prompt or summary wording.

New evidence or campaign-topic publication queues comparison; existing nightly
recovery covers missed work. Missing budget pauses before provider construction.
Automatic admission requires the explicit registered source scope and date
window; a legacy action-only policy does not authorize this stage. Shared month,
day, request, count and concurrency limits still apply. No allowance is enabled.

The campaign panel shows at most two initial records with further rows and
supporting quotes behind independent disclosures. Recorded work and issue updates
have separate labels; neither creates a personal-success claim. An unsuccessful
request can be relevant effort. No match does not prove inaction or a broken
promise. Source corrections remove stale links on later reads; current aliases
and service remain required. The all-records issue view also now includes each
documented term while excluding gaps, instead of suppressing every update.
